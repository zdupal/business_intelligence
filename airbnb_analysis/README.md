# Airbnb Analysis

#### Five Findings
1. Hosts with 10 or more listings hold **31.9%** of Chicago's listings, showing that a good amount of listings are held by larger hosts.
2. North Linden has the lowest median price per person in Columbus at **$32.60** for the listings that met the requirements.
3. The top **10%** of listings in Columbus earn **36.1%** of the city's estimated revenue, showing that a smaller group of listings earns a large portion of the revenue.
4. Columbus has the lowest share of mostly taken entire homes in October 2026 at **19%**.
5. Chicago has **267** listings with **no** availability data, which is the most out of the three cities.
